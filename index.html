Equipment Economics

The cost data behind a repair, rebuild or replace call lives in many places. People can see this month’s bill, but not the lifetime picture.

One clear recommendation is fast, but nobody acts on a number they can’t trust. The design question: how much of the math to show, and when.

  1. 1 · Spreadsheets to researchStarted from the spreadsheets teams use today. Research reshaped the approach and seeded ideas for recommendations
  2. 2 · AI-assisted workflowJoined Caterpillar’s AI initiative mid-stream and prototyped for initial user testing
  3. 3 · Living specs (current)Turned research into living specs. Claude drafts in Figma; I make the design calls
  4. OngoingContinuous questions, testing and iteration

Shown at a high level while the work is in progress. Ships early 2027, with a full case study to follow.
