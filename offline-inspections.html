1 · PROBLEM

Inspections happen where the signal doesn't

  • Who: Operators doing daily walkarounds and technicians inspecting machines on remote job sites.
  • Pain: A dropped connection meant an unfinished inspection, or no way to know if photos had uploaded.
  • Ask: In research, users wanted inspection records kept on the device until they could sync.
  • Why now: The legacy inspection app was being retired, so this moved into VisionLink.
2 · MY ROLE

Lead designer, from research to dev handoff

  • Owned: Design from February 2026 through dev handoff. Nick Guy stayed on as the app expert.
  • Influenced: The acceptance criteria, through my sessions with the product owner and mobile engineering lead.
  • System: Added offline components to a mobile design system that was still young.
3 · DECISION

Make offline a mode, not an error

  • Research: Studied Spotify, Google Translate and Letterboxd, then explored 5 banner directions in Figma Make.
  • Chose: A quiet, persistent "No internet connection" bar.
  • Slow network: A prompt asks whether to stay online or go offline.
  • Reconnect: Manual, by pulling down to refresh. Agreed with engineering.
  • Look: Deliberately distinct from the company's other inspection app.
4 · DECISION

Remove actions, don't disable them

  • Explored: Greying out the actions that don't work offline.
  • Chose: Cutting them. The offline home has only the two actions that work.
  • Why: Greyed-out buttons promise what the app can't deliver, and engineering confirmed they'd still hit the server.
  • Trade-off: Users don't see what's missing, so empty states explain what to do once back online.
5 · DECISION

Prepare online, work offline

  • Forms: Downloaded one at a time from each form's details page.
  • Compromise: I proposed separate manual and auto sections; Product wanted one list. We shipped one list with a "Downloaded" chip and an All/Downloaded toggle.
  • Assets: I designed both auto and manual sync and pushed for manual. The engineering proof of concept backed it, since auto sync meant very large requests for big fleets.
  • Size: Images are left out offline to keep downloads small.
6 · DECISION

Separate "done" from "sent"

  • Ask: Product wanted users to know when they were finished and only waiting to sync.
  • Continue: In-progress inspections show how many required questions are answered.
  • Waiting for connection: Submitted inspections, held until signal returns.
  • Safety net: Everything stays editable until it syncs.
7 · EDGE STATES

Every dead end points somewhere

If a user goes offline without preparing, each empty state says what's missing and where to get it.

Where it stands

What I'd do differently

Test in the field before build.

The design rests on heuristics, competitor patterns and an engineering proof of concept. Field sessions would have tested the slow-network prompt with real operators.

Push AI tools harder, sooner.

Figma Make was new to us and agents were just rolling out. I would prototype the connectivity logic as something people could actually use, not static frames.
