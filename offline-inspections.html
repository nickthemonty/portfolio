Overview

VisionLink is Caterpillar's fleet management platform on web and mobile. Fleet managers, equipment owners, dealer technicians and operators use it to track machine health, location, usage and inspections.

The problem

The field doesn't wait for a signal.

Operators and technicians inspect equipment where signal is weakest. Research kept saying the same thing.

"Store reports, inspections, and work documentation until connectivity is restored."Research synthesis

01 · Offline is a mode, not an error

Say it once, calmly.

Chose

A thin, persistent "No internet connection" row.

Rejected

Red error headers with a "last synced" time.

Why

Offline should feel like a state, not a failure.

Cost

Reconnecting stays manual: pull to refresh.

02 · Remove, don't disable

Show only what works.

Chose

Offline home has two actions: Start Inspection, Report a Problem.

Rejected

Greyed-out tiles labeled "Offline."

Why

Disabled features still made API calls, and led to dead ends.

Cost

Users can't see what's missing; empty states explain.

03 · Prepare while online

One list, one "Downloaded" chip.

Chose

All / Downloaded toggle; forms cache when opened; assets sync manually.

Rejected

Separate manual and auto-download lists.

Why

Big fleet lists are costly to fetch; one list is easier to scan.

Cost

Users must sync assets once before going offline.

Photos offline: saved on the device with the inspection, uploaded in the same sync when the connection returns.

04 · Separate done from sent

"Waiting to sync" is its own state.

Chose

In Progress splits into Continue and Submission Pending.

Rejected

One list with a single checkmark.

Why

People start online and finish offline; they need to know it's queued.

Cost

One more state, explained by one banner.

AI did / I decided

AI did

Figma Make drafted 5 offline banner directions. Dovetail AI pulled together past research.

I decided

Remove, don't disable. Look distinct from the legacy app. Keep done and sent separate.

What I'd do differently

  1. Field-test before build. We validated with an engineering proof of concept, not real operators
  2. Prototype the connectivity logic. A working prototype would have settled threshold debates faster than static screens
