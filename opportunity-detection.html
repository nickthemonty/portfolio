Overview

Problem

Savings were typed in one facility line at a time.

What I did

Led design end to end, then post-launch research.

Result

Shipped. 10 interviews, 38 survey responses.

Team · Time

2 POs, eng manager, 4 engineers. 3 months.

The problem

Negotiators were stuck doing data entry.

Underwriters negotiate contracts with hospital systems and enter savings across hundreds of facilities.

“I am spending 4 hours… It’s a drag on my day.”Business Analytics Senior Advisor

01 · Remove the work

Enter once, distribute everywhere.

Chose

A 5-step flow that calculates savings across every line.

Rejected

Faster line-by-line entry.

Why

Not every provider is in every deal, so users pick what’s included.

Cost

More screens; a stepper shows progress.

02 · Reuse to ship

Edit reuses the entry screens.

Chose

Edit reuses steps 4 and 5.

Rejected

A separate edit experience.

Why

Technical limits; savings change, so editing had to ship in v1.

Cost

Users can edit values, not the system or model.

03 · Fix what research found

Keep people in the flow.

Chose

Add Available and Snoozed opportunities in the workflow.

Rejected

Sending users out to change statuses.

Why

Post-launch interviews and the survey surfaced it.

Cost

Excel upload went to the roadmap, not v1.

What I’d do differently

  1. Test with underwriters before launch. The status gap only surfaced in post-launch interviews and the survey. A round of usability testing on the stepper would have caught it before release and saved an enhancement cycle
  2. Push for Excel upload in v1. Users already did their math in spreadsheets. I’d scope a simple import alongside the stepper as the MVP, not leave it as a later roadmap item
