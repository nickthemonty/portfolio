Overview

Problem

Reporters needed a safe, fast flow. The system was built for admins.

What I did

Led design for the intake flow and the new component library.

Result

Responsive library documented with components and variables.

Team · Time

UX director, 2 designers, researcher, 4 PMs, eng and architecture leads. 4 months.

The problem

Reporters aren’t admins.

Speak Up lets employees of OneTrust’s customers report ethics concerns, from small misunderstandings to fraud or abuse. Reporters are often stressed, rushed and away from a desk. OneTrust’s components were made for dense desktop admin work.

Welcoming

Reassure people the space is safe from retaliation.

Intuitive

People may have minutes. Each step is simple and hard to get lost in.

Responsive

Many report from a phone or tablet, away from the office.

01 · Extend, don’t adopt

Grow our own system, not a second one.

Chose

A new library inside OneTrust’s design system.

Rejected

Material Design, which the team first considered.

Why

Two component sets mean long-term design debt. Material also limited the branding customers needed.

Cost

We gave up Material’s ready-made docs and speed.

02 · Consumer feel, same foundation

Bigger, softer and brandable.

I extended core components instead of replacing them: larger buttons and fields, more padding, and corner radii customers can set. Mouse states and keyboard or voice focus states were designed together.

03 · A new component

Answers that explain themselves.

Reporters often need context to choose an answer. I designed a card button with a clear selected state and optional help text that each customer can write.

04 · Responsive by definition

Modals that work on a phone.

Modals had no responsive rules. I defined sizing, columns and width at each breakpoint, from small phones to large monitors, so engineers had one answer for every screen.

The mobile flow

From a chat concept to guided steps.

Early wireframes tried a chat-style flow. The final intake uses one question per step, a progress bar, and the new components.
